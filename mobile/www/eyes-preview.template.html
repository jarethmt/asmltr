What changed

No specular highlight

A hard white dot implies a glossy ball lit from outside. These are self-luminous, so the light now comes from inside — a vertical gradient, near-white at the top falling to accent at the bottom.

Shape carries emotion

Height, width, per-corner radius, bottom arc, lid angle and tilt are all animated. Happy squints by bowing the bottom edge up; concerned cuts the outer top corner down.

Speech is gestural

Envelope onsets trigger micro-saccades on emphasis; silence gaps between clauses trigger a glance away, a blink or a lean. Amplitude alone just throbs.

Springs, not lerps

Every value is a damped spring, so blinks snap and state changes overshoot slightly instead of sliding linearly.

Never perfectly still

A slow noise drift runs under gaze and eye height at all times. Perfect stillness is the strongest "cheap widget" tell there is.

One engine, three surfaces

The drawer orb, the toast and the badge all mount the same module, so they can't drift apart again.
